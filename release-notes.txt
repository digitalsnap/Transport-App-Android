RideVibe 1.0.0-beta.1

HEADS UP: this build runs on demo data. Trips, seat maps, fares, wallet
balances and tickets are all simulated locally — nothing is booked, nothing
is charged, and nothing syncs to a server. Please don't report the trip
listings or prices themselves as wrong; do report anything that looks broken
in how the app behaves.

What to try:
- Sign in with Google or Facebook
- Search for a trip and pick a date
- Choose seats on the seat map
- Run through checkout to a ticket
- Open the Scan tab and scan a ticket QR code

What we most want to hear about:
- Crashes or blank screens
- Sign-in failures (tell us which provider and which device)
- Anything slow, confusing, or visually broken on your screen size

Send feedback to: alfred@afterbodyhealth.com
